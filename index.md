# Porch Quilts Support

Porch Quilts is a calm quilt-sorting puzzle for iPhone and iPad. No ads, no timers, no lives.

## Contact

Email [linyingquan@laien.io](mailto:linyingquan@laien.io). Please include your device model, iOS/iPadOS version and the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings in the app). We usually reply within 2 business days.

## FAQ

**How do I play?**
Tap a basket to pick up its top patches, then tap a glowing basket to set them down on the same fabric or an empty spot. Four of a kind ties a bundle — bundle them all to stitch the block. Undo, Hint and Restart are free and unlimited.

**What is free?**
The first 2 quilts (24 blocks) and the Daily Block every day are free forever.

**What does Unlock Everything include?**
A one-time purchase that unlocks 10 more quilts (120 blocks), the daily Warm-up and Tricky bonus blocks, endless Sampler Quilts and all 12 fabric bundles. No subscription. It is shared with your family through Family Sharing.

**I bought Unlock Everything but it's not unlocked.**
Open **Settings** on the home screen and tap **Restore Purchases**. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

**I missed a Daily Block.**
You can catch up on any missed day of the current month from the Daily Quilt calendar.

**How do I sync between my iPhone and iPad?**
Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on both devices. Your quilts sync automatically; there is no account to create.

**Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?**
If iCloud is on, your progress is kept in your private iCloud and comes back when you reinstall with the same Apple Account. Without iCloud, progress is stored only on the device and is removed with the app. Your purchase is never lost; restore it from Settings.

**How do I turn on a daily reminder?**
Open **Settings** and turn on **Daily reminder**, then pick a time.

**Some fabrics look alike to me.**
Turn on **Stitch marks** in Settings to add a clear symbol to every fabric.

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
