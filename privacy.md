# Porch Quilts Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Porch Quilts ("the app") is developed by Yingquan Lin. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

## We do not collect your data

- The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- The app contains no third-party SDKs.
- We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

## Data stored on your device and in your iCloud

Your game progress (finished blocks and quilts, chosen fabrics, quilt labels you write) and your settings (sound, music, haptics, display options, reminder time) are saved on your device.

If you are signed in to iCloud, your game progress is also saved in your private iCloud storage using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so it can sync between your iPhone and iPad. This data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account; we cannot see or access it. See [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Daily reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device only; no notification server is involved and nothing is sent to us.

## Sharing and photos

When you tap Share, the app creates a picture of your quilt or block and hands it to the iOS share sheet. It is sent only to the destination you choose. If you choose Save Image, the picture is added to your photo library. The app never reads your photo library.

## In-app purchases

"Unlock Everything" is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app only asks Apple whether the purchase has been made so it can unlock the content.

## Crash reports

If you choose to share crash reports and app analytics with developers in your device settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports. They do not identify you.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
